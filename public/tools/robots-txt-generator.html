What should an AI-ready robots.txt contain?

An AI-ready robots.txt explicitly allows the crawlers that power AI answers: OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User (ChatGPT), PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot, Googlebot (AI Overviews) and Bingbot (Copilot). It still disallows private paths like admin or checkout pages and points to your sitemap.

  • A crawler obeys only the most specific group that names it. If you add a group for OAI-SearchBot, it ignores the User-agent: * rules, so we copy your private paths into each group.
  • Training bots like GPTBot and Google-Extended can be blocked without affecting AI search visibility.
  • Firewalls can block bots even when robots.txt allows them. Test with the crawler checker after uploading.
