Why don't AI engines cite some websites?

AI answer engines now decide which businesses get recommended. Most invisible sites fail for reasons that are easy to fix but impossible for the owner to see:

The site blocks AI crawlers without anyone knowing

A firewall, a CDN "block AI bots" toggle or an old robots.txt rule can stop OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot or Claude-SearchBot. We test robots.txt and the live response.

The page is empty without JavaScript

Many AI crawlers read raw HTML only. A client-side React or app-shell site can look blank to them. We show exactly what the bot sees.

The site doesn't say who the business is

Without Organization schema, sameAs links and clear titles, a model can't connect the website to the brand it's asked about.

The content isn't quotable

No direct answers, lists, tables or dates. Structured, answer-first, dated pages are the ones AI engines lift and cite.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI search audit?

An AI search audit checks whether AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews can reach a website, understand who the business is, and quote its content. CiteReady runs 18 rule-based checks on the live site and gives a 0–100 score with a copy-paste fix for every problem.

Why can't ChatGPT see my website?

The most common reasons are a robots.txt rule or firewall that blocks OAI-SearchBot or ChatGPT-User, content that only appears after JavaScript runs, a noindex tag, or missing schema that explains who the business is.

Should I block GPTBot?

That's a business choice. GPTBot collects data to train OpenAI models. Blocking it does not remove a site from ChatGPT search answers, which use OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User. CiteReady reports training bots but never lowers your score for blocking them.

How is the score calculated?

The score is the sum of 16 weighted checks in three groups: Can AI reach it (40 points), Can AI understand it (35 points) and Will AI quote it (25 points). Every point links to the evidence that produced it.

Can I put my agency's logo on the report?

Yes. White-label PDF reports with your logo, colours and agency name are included from the first paid plan ($29/month) or as a $9 single report.

Does llms.txt help AI search rankings?

No major AI search engine has confirmed using llms.txt yet, so CiteReady reports it as low impact and gives it zero points. We still generate a draft because it's cheap to add.
