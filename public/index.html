What's the difference between Readiness and model recognition?

Readiness is a technical audit of what on the website stops AI crawlers from reaching, understanding or quoting it. Model recognition is a separate signal: whether open AI models already know the brand. Neither guarantees that a specific AI service will cite a page.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI search audit?

An AI search audit checks whether AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews can reach a website, understand who the business is, and quote its content. CiteSeen runs 18 checks (16 scored, 2 informational) and gives a copy-paste fix for every problem it can fix.

What is the difference between the Readiness score and AI model recognition?

The Readiness score measures what's fixable on the website itself. AI model recognition asks open AI models (hosted on NVIDIA NIM, no web browsing) the questions customers ask, three times each, and measures how often they name the brand from what they already know. It is not a measurement of ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google results.

Why can't ChatGPT see my website?

The most common reasons are a robots.txt rule or firewall that blocks OAI-SearchBot or ChatGPT-User, content that only appears after JavaScript runs, a noindex tag, or missing schema that explains who the business is.

Should I block GPTBot?

That's a business choice. GPTBot collects data to train OpenAI models. Blocking it does not remove a site from ChatGPT search answers, which use OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User. CiteSeen reports training bots but never lowers your score for blocking them.

Which AI models run the model recognition checks?

Open models hosted on NVIDIA NIM. Pro uses the largest models, Plus mid-size models and Free small, fast models. They answer from training data and don't browse the web, so results show how well AI already knows a brand, not live search citations.

Can I put my agency's logo on the report?

Yes. White-label PDF reports with your logo, colour and agency name are included on Plus and Pro, or as a $9 single report.
