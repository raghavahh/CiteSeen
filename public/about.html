About CiteReady

CiteReady is a fix-first AI search audit for small SEO, web and marketing agencies. It shows why a website is invisible to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI, gives the exact code to fix it, and proves the fix worked.

Why does CiteReady exist?

Agencies are being asked to deliver "AI search visibility", but the tools that produce client-ready reports cost $199–$385 a month, and most of them only monitor. They tell you a brand is missing from AI answers, not what to change on the site. CiteReady does the opposite: every finding ships with a copy-paste fix generated from the site's own data.

What do we believe?

  • Fixes over dashboards. A problem without a fix is just a worry.
  • Transparent scoring. Every point on the score links to the check and evidence that produced it.
  • Honest about uncertainty. When a test can give a false alarm, like a firewall that verifies bots by IP, we say "possible" and show how to confirm it.
  • Your choice on AI training. Blocking training bots is a legitimate decision and never lowers your score.

Who builds it?

CiteReady is built by Pithani Raghavendra, an independent founder in India. Questions, feedback and partnership ideas are welcome on the contact page.

How does the audit request your site?

Our auditor identifies itself as CiteReadyAudit when reading robots.txt, sitemaps and llms.txt. For the firewall test it also requests the page using the published user agents of AI search bots, once per audit, so we can compare what they receive with what a browser receives.
