Privacy policy

Effective: 14 October 2026

What we collect

  • Audit requests: the website address you enter and the audit result. Results are stored for 30 days so the share link works, then deleted automatically.
  • Early-access signups: your email address and, if you give them, your agency name and number of clients, plus the country derived from your connection.
  • Rate limiting: your IP address is used for about an hour to stop abuse, then discarded.

We don't use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

How we use it

  • To run audits and show results.
  • To email you about early access and the launch offer. Every email has an unsubscribe option.
  • To produce anonymous, aggregated statistics, for example the share of websites that block an AI crawler. These never identify you.

Where it's stored

CiteSeen runs on Cloudflare's network. Data is stored with Cloudflare and, once paid plans launch, with our payment providers (PayPal, Razorpay) and a transactional email service, each under their own privacy terms.

Your choices

Email hello@citeseen.app to see, correct or delete your data, or to ask us not to audit your domain.
